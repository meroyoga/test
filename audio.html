Yes. You can return to the audio and listen again. You might notice something different each time.

Do I need to prepare anything?

No special preparation is needed. Find a comfortable space, give yourself some uninterrupted time, and have your workbook nearby if you'd like to use it.

What if I need to pause?

That's absolutely fine. You can pause the recording and return when you're ready. Move through the experience at a pace that feels right for you.
