Scott's approach

Wake up more deeply. Live more fully.

Scott Schwenk

Meditation teacher, somatic healing guide and master coach bringing depth, warmth, and embodied practical insight to the work of living with greater presence, resiliency, adaptability, and love.

Scott Schwenk brings practicality, compassion, and a sense of discovery to the work of waking up, growing up, cleaning up, and showing up. For more than 30 years, he has supported the inner evolution and leadership development of individuals and organizations around the world.

His teaching draws on years spent living and studying in a Hindu-Tantric monastery, along with long-term training and apprenticeships in meditation, somatic healing, shadow work, attachment healing, Eastern philosophy, and leadership development. Stillness, breath, contemplation, and laughter all have a place in his approach.

The invitation is to explore how understanding becomes a way of living: how we listen, meet difficulty, care for one another, act with integrity and thrive across all dimensions of life. This app brings Scott's recorded teachings into an ongoing practice you can return to as life changes.

Begin where you are

A few thoughtful ways to begin exploring your practice. Find what speaks to you and take the next step at your own pace.

Explore what meditation and breathwork can offer. Begin with a simple practice and discover what feels relevant to your life right now.
